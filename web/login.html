Sign in

A login form for trying Passport. Nothing you type leaves this page: there is no account and no server behind it.

  1. In Passport, click New and save an entry with URL https://passport.plu.nz/login, a username and a password.
  2. Open this page in Brave or Chrome, open the Passport extension and pick the entry to fill the form.
  3. Click Sign in to check what was filled.

Any username and password are accepted. The form is checked in your browser only.
